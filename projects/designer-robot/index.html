Designer Robot

A robotic platform being developed for precision food design, controlled deposition and digital-to-physical manufacturing workflows.

Development project
Development stage
In development — prototype engineering and evaluation are ongoing. Not commercially available.
Engineering disciplines
RoboticsMechanical designMotion controlProcess engineeringSoftware workflows

01/ChallengeChallenge

Creative food design — decorations, patterns and shaped deposits — is often manual, difficult to reproduce and hard to connect to digital design tools. The challenge is to translate a digital design into precise, repeatable physical deposition of edible materials.

02/ApproachApproach

  1. 01Define a motion architecture suited to accurate positioning over a working surface.
  2. 02Engineer a controlled deposition system for materials with different flow behaviour.
  3. 03Build a digital workflow that converts a design file into robot motion and dosing commands.
  4. 04Iterate through prototypes to evaluate motion, deposition and usability.
