Simulation-Driven Engineering

An illustration of how thermal, structural and fluid simulation are combined to inform engineering decisions before physical prototyping.

Illustrative technical showcase

Engineering disciplines
Heat transferComputational fluid dynamicsFinite element analysisMultiphysics modeling

01/ChallengeChallenge

Design choices such as material thickness, cooling geometry or nozzle shape are often made by trial and error. Computational simulation helps to understand the underlying physics and compare options virtually.

02/ApproachApproach

  1. 01Formulate the engineering question the simulation must answer.
  2. 02Build coupled models for heat transfer, structural loading and flow as needed.
  3. 03Verify numerical models and compare with experimental data when available.
  4. 04Translate results into concrete design recommendations.
