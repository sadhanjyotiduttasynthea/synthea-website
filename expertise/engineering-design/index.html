05/FAQFAQ

Questions, answered.

Ask us directly
Which file formats can you deliver?

Native CAD files and neutral formats such as STEP, along with PDF drawings. Tell us what your manufacturer uses and we will align with it.

Can you start from a sketch?

Yes. Many projects start from a sketch, a photo or a verbal description. The first phase clarifies requirements and feasibility.

Do you help with manufacturing?

We prepare documentation for manufacturing and can support discussions with your suppliers and the assembly of prototypes.

Who owns the design?

Intellectual property terms are agreed in writing for each project before work begins.
