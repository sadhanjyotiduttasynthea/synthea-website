05/FAQFAQ

Questions, answered.

Ask us directly
Do you integrate with existing production lines?

We can design equipment and interfaces intended to fit existing lines. Integration requirements are reviewed at the start of each project.

Can you develop software as well as hardware?

Yes, for engineering interfaces, motion sequences and digital workflows connected to the equipment we design.

Is automation worthwhile for small production volumes?

Sometimes. A short feasibility study helps determine whether partial automation, tooling or full automation makes sense.

How do you handle safety?

Safety is considered from the concept stage. Compliance and certification requirements depend on the application and are scoped explicitly.
